Go-live plumbing — no Slack yet

  • Nothing stamps went_live_at today. The notifier was designed in 0019's header, built, then archived; MIGRATION_DONE_STAGE still exists with no caller.
  • updateProcess will stamp it once, idempotently, when lifecycle flips to live or migration_stage to live_on_v2, and append a MILESTONE event — the first process-side writer events has had.
  • Processes with no customer get the stamp but no event, since appendEvent requires a customer key and customer_id is nullable.
  • Migration 0044 backfills went_live_at from go_live_date for existing live rows, so history isn't empty on day one.
  • No Slack post in this change. Wiring it later is one call at the stamp site plus MIGRATION_SLACK_CHANNEL; the archived template has the copy.
