Holds up well

  • Status colors (green/amber) pop cleanly against dark — arguably easier to scan than on the light glass version
  • Yellow stays rare and meaningful (tab underline, one badge, one accent) — doesn't get noisy in a 20-row table

Watch for

  • All-dark for 8+ hours of screen time is a real ergonomics question — recommend dark as the primary/default with a light toggle kept, not dark-only
  • Charts (Analytics page) need a matching dark palette — more design work than tables/cards, flagged for the rollout stage
